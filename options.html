🔗 Google Integration

Connect to your Google Sheet and Drive folder for data storage.

Find it in the Sheet URL: docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/{THIS_PART}/edit
Find it in the Drive folder URL: drive.google.com/drive/folders/{THIS_PART}

📘 Meta Integration

Optional — used to auto-detect ad accounts with spend.

Find it in any Meta Business URL: business.facebook.com/latest/...?business_id=THIS_PART
Leave blank to manually enter account IDs. Only needed for auto-detect feature.
